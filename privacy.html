Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Atlas Assistant ("Atlas") is a private tool used solely by its owner, Keneth-Ryan De Souza, with his own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, Atlas accesses the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar data through Google APIs to summarize email, identify deadlines, show schedule conflicts, draft or send messages the owner approves, and create or edit calendar events the owner approves.

Use and storage

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Atlas does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train generalized AI models. Summaries may be stored locally on the owner's computer. OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

Atlas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
